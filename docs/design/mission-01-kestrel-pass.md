# Mission 1: Kestrel Pass

Status: design for checklist item 4.1. The compound arena is the prototype of this mission's second half; this document describes the full vertical-slice mission that replaces it once terrain (3.3) and the environment kit (3.4) exist.

## Fantasy

A lone marksman overwatches a mountain pass at dawn, picks apart a supply outpost from a ridge 600 m away, then closes in to recover a ledger and leave unseen. It teaches the player that position, patience and the first shot decide everything.

## Setting

Fictional border region, late autumn, clear dawn turning windy by mid-mission. Fictional faction: the Varn Accord, a private security force guarding a fuel and arms depot at the head of the pass. No real units, places or brands.

## Story context

This is the first full mission after the playable prologue, **The Last Quiet Night**. Elias Vance (Echo) has survived the attack on his home and learned that the Varn Accord carried it out. He follows a trace from the damaged Black Ledger to this depot, believing the unit's records will identify who authorized the strike.

The depot commander is a local Accord officer, not Commander Rusk, who led the raid on Elias's home. Rusk is not killed in this mission; his authorization signature and a short encrypted command recording are among the evidence recovered from the office. This preserves Rusk as a recurring field antagonist and makes the mission's discovery more important than a simple revenge kill.

Mara Quill provides the briefing and radio support. Her dialogue should be restrained: she knows this is personal, but insists that Elias bring back proof. After the ledger is recovered, Imani Rook can decode the first fragment and identify the name **NIGHTGLASS**. Silas Vey should be foreshadowed through a corporate authorization mark or clipped broadcast, not introduced in a long exposition dump.

## Narrative beats

1. **Briefing:** Mara explains that the depot supplied the team that hit Elias's home. The target is the records office, not indiscriminate revenge.
2. **Approach:** Environmental details—fresh vehicle tracks, a damaged Accord transport and a hurriedly cleared office—show the site was used recently.
3. **Discovery:** The ledger fragment links the raid to a larger operation and carries Rusk's authorization signature.
4. **Complication:** If the radio mast remains active, the Accord attempts to lock down the pass and destroy the remaining records. Disabling it buys time and changes the extraction pressure.
5. **Extraction:** Mara confirms that the recovered evidence is authentic. Imani identifies NIGHTGLASS, setting up Echo Ridge.

Dialogue should react to the player's approach (undetected, alarm raised, mast disabled) without changing the core discovery. Keep the mission's quiet moments short and let the environment carry part of the story.

## Map (about 1 km²)

| Area | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Insertion gully (south) | Safe start, tutorial reminders, first view of the pass |
| Kestrel Ridge (west, 80 m above the depot) | Primary overwatch: 450 to 650 m to the depot, wind funnels down the pass |
| Pine shelf (east) | Alternative overwatch, 300 to 400 m, closer but exposed to two watchtowers |
| The depot (centre north) | Walled compound: two huts, fuel tanks, a radio mast, the ledger in the office |
| Culvert (under the road) | Quiet route into the depot for players who clear the towers |
| Extraction (north saddle) | Reached through the depot or around it on the high trail |

## Objectives

| Kind | Objective | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Eliminate the depot commander | Patrols between the office and the mast on a 90 s loop |
| Primary | Recover the ledger from the office | Reveals once the commander is down or the office is reached |
| Primary | Reach the north saddle | Exfil trigger zone |
| Secondary | Disable the radio mast before lockdown | Shooting the mast's junction box stops reinforcements |
| Secondary | Stay undetected | Fails on squad Alert |
| Optional | Kill the commander from beyond 500 m | Rewards Kestrel Ridge |
| Optional | Fire only suppressed weapons | Same rule as the compound |

## Three approaches

1. **Overwatch.** Take Kestrel Ridge, range the depot, read the gusting wind from the flags, take the commander at 550 m, then pick off the tower guards before they relocate. Hardest shots, safest position. Lockdown is likely unless the mast goes first.
2. **Shelf and culvert.** Clear the two towers from the pine shelf at 350 m, slip through the culvert, take the ledger, and leave through the saddle. Medium shots, more movement and noise management.
3. **Ghost.** Never fire the rifle. Binoculars and tags, stones to pull the gate guard, the culvert, a suppressed sidearm only on the commander at close range, and the high trail out.

## Enemies

Eight guards on patrol routes plus a commander. Two tower guards scan with wider vision cones. Lockdown adds a four-guard truck at the north gate unless the mast is down. Dusk-level visibility is not used; this is a dawn-into-day mission, so the tension comes from distance and wind rather than darkness.

## Systems it exercises

Ballistics at 300 to 650 m with gusting crosswind, zero turret use, rangefinder and holdover, stances and breath on exposed ridges, perception and squad alerts, body carrying in the depot, stones, objectives with triggers, checkpoints at the ridge and the culvert, the results screen and all three relevant achievements.

## What it needs that does not exist yet

- Terrain and environment kit (3.3, 3.4) for the ridge, shelf and pass.
- A shootable radio mast junction box (small new target type).
- A truck reinforcement spawn path (navigation along the road).
- Flag props that visualise the gusting wind.
- Ambient audio and weapon audio (3.10) for the shots to carry weight.

## Acceptance

A playtester new to the game finishes it in each of the three approaches across three runs without being told the approaches exist, and rates the commander shot as the highlight.
