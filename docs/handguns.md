# Echo Ridge — handgun system

## Playable handgun lineup

All names and tuning are fictional game content. Handguns use the existing hitscan weapon path, independent magazines and reserve ammunition, weapon switching, aim FOV, recoil feedback, reload timers, hit zones, impact feedback, and the existing suppression flag.

| Weapon | Role | Magazine | Reserve | Gameplay feel |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Whisper sidearm | Quiet close-range option | 15 | 45 | Suppressed, quick follow-up shots, lower damage |
| Sentinel service pistol | Balanced standard sidearm | 17 | 51 | Reliable general-purpose pistol |
| Ironclad heavy pistol | High-impact sidearm | 10 | 40 | Slower follow-up shots, stronger recoil, higher damage |

The rifle remains slot 1. The three handguns occupy slots 2–4 and can also be reached by cycling the weapon selection action. Each profile has its own ammunition pool and cooldown state. The existing sidearm gunsmith family applies compatible fictional handling/optic parts across the handgun lineup.

## Controls

- 1: rifle
- 2: Whisper sidearm
- 3: Sentinel service pistol
- 4: Ironclad heavy pistol
- Q / configured next-weapon action: cycle through the loadout
- Right mouse / configured aim action: aim
- R: reload
- Left mouse / configured fire action: fire

## Existing systems reused

- Hitscan firing, hit-zone damage and impact events
- Independent ammo and reserve tracking per weapon
- Timed reloads, partial reload conservation and firing cooldowns
- Aim FOV changes and weapon recoil feedback
- Suppressor state for the Whisper sidearm
- Gunsmith profile application and saved sidearm-family configuration

## Production art and audio still required

This branch adds game data and loadout integration; it does not claim finished first-person handgun models, hand/arm rigs, reload/fire animations, slide movement, shell ejection, muzzle-flash variants, or recorded handgun audio. Those need licensed or original assets and an in-game art/audio pass. No paid assets are required by this data-only addition.
